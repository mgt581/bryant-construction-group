Questions about this service

Do you handle small plaster patches as well as rooms?

The existing service covers plaster repairs and patch work alongside wall and ceiling skimming. Send the approximate size and photographs of the area for an initial discussion.

When can a repaired wall be decorated?

The surface needs to dry and be ready for the chosen finish. Timing depends on the repair and conditions, so discuss preparation and decorating as part of the job rather than assuming a fixed drying period.
