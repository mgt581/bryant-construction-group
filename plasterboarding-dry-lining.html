Questions about this service

Do you tape and joint or skim?

Both. Taping and jointing gives a paint-ready finish without wet plaster, while a full skim suits areas that need a harder, more uniform surface. We can talk through which suits your room.

Can you board loft and sloped ceilings?

Yes. Sloped ceilings, dormers and bulkheads are a regular part of our boarding work. Send photos of the space so we can see the angles and access.
