Questions about this service

Can you look at several small repairs together?

Yes. Send one list of the door adjustments, trim repairs, wall patches or finishing jobs you need. We can discuss the scope together rather than treating each item as a separate enquiry.

What should I send for a repair quote?

Include your postcode, a description of each problem and photos if available. Mention access restrictions and whether the property is occupied. Photos help with the initial discussion; some work needs a visit before a quote.
