Questions about this service

Do I need drawings before getting a quote?

For a firm price, yes — approved drawings and any structural calculations let us quote the work accurately. Before that, we can give you an idea of what is involved.

Do you handle planning permission?

Planning and building regulations approvals are applied for by the homeowner or their designer. We work to the approved drawings and can tell you what information we need from them.
