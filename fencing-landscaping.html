Questions about this service

Can you replace just a few damaged panels?

Yes. We repair sections of fence as well as full runs. If the posts have failed, we will recommend replacing them so the new panels stay secure.

Concrete or timber posts?

Concrete posts with gravel boards last longer and resist rot. Timber posts suit some designs and closeboard fences. We will explain the options when we quote.
