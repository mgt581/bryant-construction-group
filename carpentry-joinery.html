Questions about this service

Can I enquire about door adjustments and trim repairs?

Yes. Doors, frames, door furniture, skirting boards and architraves are part of the existing carpentry service. Include photos and explain whether you need a repair, adjustment or replacement.

What should I provide for fitted storage?

Send approximate measurements, photos of the space and what you want to store. These details help start the discussion; final dimensions and fitting details need to be agreed before work begins.
