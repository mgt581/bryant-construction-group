Questions about this service

Can decorating include surface preparation?

The decorating service includes filling, sanding, masking and protecting the work area. Describe damaged surfaces in your enquiry so the preparation needed can be discussed.

What if a wall needs plaster repairs first?

Send photos of the damage with your enquiry. Our plastering and finishing page explains the patch repair and preparation work available before decorating.
