Questions about this service

Can you match my existing bricks?

We try to source bricks that closely match in colour and size. On older walls an exact match is not always possible, so we will talk you through the options before starting.

Should a leaning wall be repaired or rebuilt?

It depends on the cause and how far it has moved. Photos let us give an initial view, and we will confirm once we have seen it in person.
