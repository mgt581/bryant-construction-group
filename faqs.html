Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about quotes, response times, guarantee cover and how we work.

Need a direct answer?

If your question is not covered here, contact Bryant Construction Group and we’ll point you in the right direction. We keep the process clear, practical and straightforward.

How do I get a quote?

Send us the job details, your location and any photos you have. We’ll review the request and provide a clear quote.

What areas do you cover?

We cover Bournemouth, Poole, Christchurch and nearby areas for building, repairs, maintenance, carpentry, decorating and plastering work.

Do you offer a workmanship guarantee?

Yes. Eligible completed works are covered by our workmanship guarantee. Installation issues are included under the terms of that cover.

How fast do you respond?

We aim to respond quickly to quote requests and usually within 48 hours for eligible workmanship issues that need review.

What type of jobs do you take on?

We handle general building, repairs and maintenance, carpentry and joinery, decorating and plastering / finishing work.

Are you insured?

Yes. We are fully insured and work to a professional standard on every job.

Does the guarantee cover materials or manufacturer faults?

No. Our workmanship guarantee covers installation issues only and excludes normal wear and tear, misuse, manufacturer faults or third-party work.
