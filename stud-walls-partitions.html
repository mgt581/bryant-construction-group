Stud walls & partitions in Bournemouth

Split a room, create a new bedroom or office, or box in services with a properly built stud wall.

What we do

We build timber and metal stud walls for homes, rental properties and commercial spaces. Each wall can include doors, insulation for sound and fire-rated board where needed, boarded and finished ready to decorate.

  • Timber stud walls and room splits
  • Metal stud partitions for offices and shops
  • Door openings and linings
  • Acoustic insulation between rooms
  • Boxing-in and bulkheads

Questions about this service

How long does a stud wall take?

A straightforward room split is usually built and boarded in a few days, followed by taping, jointing or skimming. We confirm timings when we quote.

Can you soundproof the new wall?

We can fill the wall with acoustic insulation and use denser board to reduce noise between rooms. Tell us if sound is a priority.
